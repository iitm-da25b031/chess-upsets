Every upset at the 10% level (11,114 games: 9,007 classical, 1,483 rapid, 624 blitz) plotted at its rating gap and average rating; an upset is defined by the empirical model: the underdog won although the model gave it P(win) < 10%, or drew although P(not losing) < 10% (604 of the upsets are such draws). The 20 rarest (smallest model probability, q from 0.0136 to 0.0205) are highlighted and listed; all of them are classical, with gaps of 626-1218 points. Limitation: this shows where upsets occur, not how likely they are (dense regions are dense mainly because many games are played there), and the empty band along the left edge is built into the definition (where the model gives the underdog 10% or more, no result counts as an upset); a very large gap often means an outdated or wrong rating rather than a sensational game; full links are in rarest_upsets.csv.
