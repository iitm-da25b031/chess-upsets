Observed classical results of the lower-rated player against the rating gap (50-point bins), pooled (left) and split (middle, right; the light grey line repeats the pooled curve). At gaps of 50-199 the underdog scores 0.407 points per game with White but 0.328 with Black (win rate 21.9% vs 16.4%); between near-equal players (gap of 25 or less) the draw rate rises with the rating band from 33% (<2200) to 49% (2200-2499) and 63% (2500+), so at the same gap a strong underdog wins less often (11.5% vs 22.2% at 50-199 for 2500+ vs <2200) but draws far more. Limitation: the band is defined by the average rating, so bands also differ in their gap mix and in who plays (juniors, open events); colour and band are shown one at a time, not jointly, and bins with fewer than 100 games are not drawn.
