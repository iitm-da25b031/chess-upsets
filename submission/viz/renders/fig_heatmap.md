Each cell is the observed share of games won by the lower-rated player (draws count as not winning), by 50-point gap bin and 100-point bin of the average rating (both colours pooled); the lower row counts the games per cell. The green step marks, per rating row, the first gap bin whose win rate is below 10%: in classical it sits at a median gap of 350 points for average ratings 1600-1999 but 200 for 2200-2599. Limitation: cells with fewer than 100 games are hatched (the coloured cells hold 99.4% of the games shown); the step is the first crossing of a noisy observed rate, not a fitted boundary, and draws, which rise with rating, are ignored.
