Observed underdog win rate (markers, Wilson 95% intervals) per 50-point gap bin against the model's like-for-like prediction (line: mean of each game's own predicted P(win), using its own gap and average rating), by rating band and time control. In the 229 bins with at least 100 games the prediction lies inside the observed interval in 193 (84%; about 95% would be expected if the model were exact), and the n-weighted mean error per panel and colour ranges from -2.0 to +0.6 percentage points (largest: classical, 2500+, white underdog, -2.0 pp). Limitation: the prediction is in-sample (the model was fitted to these games; the held-out check is in the calibration figure), the intervals ignore the clustering of games in tournaments, and evaluating the curve at one reference rating instead would shift the prediction by at most 0.27 pp in any well-filled bin of the 2200-2499 band (up to 4.9 pp in the wide <2200 band). The clearest misfit is classical 2500+, where underdogs win less often than predicted (model inside the interval in only 5 of 14 bins); the interaction model (rating x gap terms) does not remove it (5 of 14 bins, -2.2 pp; see fig_model_misfit).
