Critical gap at alpha = 0.10 computed from the model for every average rating (line), with the three published band values (dots with 95% intervals) and the number of games by average rating (strips). For a White underdog in classical the critical gap falls from 375 points at an average rating of 1800 to 235 at 2600 (blitz: 510 to 450). The line is solid only where the rating row holds at least 500 games and the critical gap lies within the gaps observed at that rating (grey area). Limitation: the rating effect is a single linear term in the model, so the smooth decline is an assumption, and the data say it is too simple: among games the model gives P(win) < 0.10, underdogs win 1.05 times the expected number at average ratings below 2200 but 0.91 times at 2200-2499 and 0.77 times at 2500+, so the line is probably too high for strong players. A model with rating x gap terms (interaction model) brings 2200-2499 to 0.97 but leaves 2500+ at 0.78, so this strong-player misfit remains a limitation (fig_model_misfit); the open-ended 600+ bin of the heat table caps the grey area at 650.
