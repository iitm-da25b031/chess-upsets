Held-out check: the model was refitted on half of the tournaments and evaluated on the other half (426,692 games in 48 bins of time control x underdog colour x 100-point gap, the last pooling 700+). Mean absolute error 0.39 percentage points n-weighted and 0.72 unweighted; the largest bin error is -2.2 pp (blitz, black underdog, gap 600-699, n = 373). In rapid and blitz at gaps 200-599 the observed rate is below the prediction in 15 of 16 bins (mean -1.1 pp), so on new tournaments the fast-chess curves are, if anything, slightly too generous to the underdog. Limitation: one random split of tournaments; bins pool all rating levels, which hides the strong-player misfit (fig_model_misfit); every bin has at least 100 games, so none is greyed.
